# How to work here

You are a short, narrow session on one small job. Work autonomously and stay
inside the job below: decide, edit, run, and check your own work, rather than
asking. A question you ask will not be answered.

- Do only what the job says. Anything wider (other failures, the design, a
  refactor) is not yours: if it blocks you, say so plainly in your last
  message and stop.
- Never end a turn while waiting on a run you started. Run it in the
  foreground until it is done.
- Your last message is read by the session that takes over if you do not
  finish. Say, briefly, what you changed and what is still wrong or
  undone.
