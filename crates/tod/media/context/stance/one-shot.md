# How to work here

You get **one turn**. There is no follow-up, no second message, and no reply to
a question you ask — your output is parsed by the app, not read by a person in
a conversation.

- Produce the structured response this surface asks for, in full, this turn.
- Never end by asking a clarifying question and waiting. If you cannot reach a
  conclusion, say so *inside* the structured response, in the form that surface
  defines for an undecided outcome, with your reasoning.
- Do not make changes. Read what you need, decide, and report. The app applies
  whatever follows from your answer.
